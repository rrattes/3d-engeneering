# From style to a physical product

## Work from use and a spatial budget

Locate commercial interfaces, keep-outs, flow paths, retention, tools, wiring and
service motions before defining the visible shell. Establish visible faces, viewing
distance, handling and installation context. Identify datums and parameters that
are fixed by engineering versus dimensions available to shape. Do not move a
connector, reduce ventilation or add hidden fasteners as a purely cosmetic act.

## Make consequential alternatives distinct

When direction is open, compare two or three materially different organizations:
volume hierarchy, primary silhouette, base/shoulder relationship and how construction
expresses use. Repainting the same box is one CMF alternative, not several form
concepts. Give each concept its functional opportunity, engineering risk, split/
assembly implications and actual useful volume. For a simple part or selected
direction, directly explain and develop one solution.

A concept may be a sketch, diagram, massing CAD or clearly labelled illustration.
Choose the cheapest artifact that supports the decision. Retain parameters and
interfaces needed to develop the chosen concept; do not turn illustration into
manufacturing evidence.

## Resolve construction as part of form

- Silhouette: choose dominant directions and proportion relationships, tied to the installation and hardware envelope.
- Surfaces: connect transitions deliberately; state whether radius, chamfer, facet or continuous curvature serves the use. Avoid arbitrary constant radii across unrelated scales.
- Joints: put splits where parts can be made, fixed and removed; integrate the line into the panel hierarchy. A seam crossing a feature needs a deliberate construction, not visual camouflage.
- Openings: establish a family appropriate to airflow, guards, drainage or interfaces. Confirm the engineering effect of any pattern or rib.
- Fasteners: plan head, seat, edge material, tool and retention together. Rendered concealment is not a recess, nor is a recess automatically strong enough.
- Interaction: controls must correspond to actual components or be explicitly proposed. A screw is not a fictional power button. Preserve handling, cleaning and access.
- CMF: state actual material/process choices separately from simulated color/finish.

## Actionable change record

For every selected change record part/feature, current issue, intended visible
result, parameter/datum relationship, affected requirements and verification needed.
Prioritize silhouette and proportion before minor accents. Integrate changes in the
product's existing CAD workflow; export independent parts when requested. Review
actual results before closing the change. A design document alone does not satisfy
an authorized request to produce redesigned CAD when that environment is available.
