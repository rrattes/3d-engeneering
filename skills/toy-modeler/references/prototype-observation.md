# Observe a toy prototype

Use a short, low-cost session appropriate to the intended player and context. Do not script the desired behavior so tightly that it hides confusion.

Record:

- What the person noticed and tried first.
- Where they held or manipulated the toy; any awkward reach or grip.
- What response they noticed (movement, pose, sound, click, reveal, or transformation).
- Whether they tried again, changed the action, or invented another use.
- Where the toy stalled, came apart, or failed to respond as expected.

Separate direct observations, participant comments, and designer interpretation. Convert findings into one concrete iteration at a time, then rerun affected CAD/motion checks. If there is no physical prototype or user observation, label the experience as a design hypothesis.
